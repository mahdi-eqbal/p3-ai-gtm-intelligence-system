# ICP Definition

## Target Company Profile

Primary ICP:

- B2B SaaS
- 50–1000 employees
- North America or EMEA
- active GTM / Sales / RevOps motion
- meaningful software / automation usage
- evidence of growth, operational complexity, or GTM change

## Strong Fit Signals

Examples:

- 100–500 employees
- SaaS / software / AI / technology-enabled business
- active hiring in Sales, RevOps, GTM, Operations, or Growth
- recent expansion or funding
- visible CRM / automation / data-stack complexity
- multiple sales or marketing motions
- international or multi-segment GTM

## Moderate Fit

Examples:

- 50–99 employees
- 500–1000 employees
- adjacent technology-enabled industry
- relevant GTM structure but weaker immediate signal

## Weak Fit

Examples:

- limited commercial complexity
- very small team
- weak B2B motion
- little evidence of structured sales operations

## Exclusions

- consumer-only businesses
- companies with no meaningful B2B commercial motion
- obvious agencies / consultancies unless specifically targeted
- businesses outside supported regions
