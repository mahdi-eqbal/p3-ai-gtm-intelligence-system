# Prioritization Model

## Score Components

### ICP Fit Score

Strong Fit = 40
Moderate Fit = 25
Weak Fit = 10
Disqualified = 0

### Signal Score

Very strong recent signal = 30
Strong signal = 22
Moderate signal = 12
Weak / stale signal = 5
No useful signal = 0

### Evidence Score

High-quality evidence = 20
Medium-quality evidence = 12
Low-quality evidence = 5
No evidence = 0

### Timing Score

Signal within 30 days = 10
31–90 days = 7
91–180 days = 3
Older / unknown = 0

## Total

Maximum score = 100

## Priority Tiers

P1 = 80–100
P2 = 60–79
P3 = 40–59
Review = below 40 or confidence exception

## Guardrail

A high numerical score does not override low-confidence or conflicting evidence.

Low-confidence high-score accounts must be sent to Review.
