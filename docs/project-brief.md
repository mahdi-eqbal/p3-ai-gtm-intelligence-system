# Project 3 — AI-Assisted Account Research & GTM Intelligence System

## Project Type

Independent professional GTM Engineering / Revenue Systems implementation and synthetic case study.

## Business Problem

GTM teams frequently research accounts manually across fragmented sources.

This creates:

- inconsistent account research
- weak prioritization
- unverified AI-generated claims
- poor evidence traceability
- generic messaging
- wasted sales research time
- difficulty distinguishing real buying signals from noise

## System Goal

Design and implement an AI-assisted GTM intelligence system that:

1. receives a target account list
2. enriches company data
3. collects relevant GTM and market signals
4. separates evidence from inference
5. evaluates ICP fit
6. measures signal relevance and confidence
7. identifies likely buyer personas
8. produces evidence-backed account intelligence
9. generates structured messaging briefs
10. prioritizes accounts for human review and sales action

## Primary Platform

Clay

## AI Layer

Claude

## Supporting Stack

- Clay
- Claude
- n8n
- REST APIs
- JavaScript
- structured JSON outputs

## Design Principle

AI output must be evidence-aware.

The system must distinguish:

- confirmed facts
- inferred hypotheses
- uncertain claims

Low-confidence or unsupported claims must not be presented as verified facts.

## Portfolio Positioning

This project demonstrates AI-enabled GTM Engineering across enrichment, research automation, structured reasoning, evidence control, prioritization, and sales intelligence.

No client, employer, production deployment, commercial outcome, or paid engagement is claimed.
