# Account Research Schema

Each account should produce a structured research record.

## Company Identity

- company_name
- domain
- headquarters
- country
- employee_count
- industry
- business_model

## ICP Assessment

- icp_fit
- icp_fit_reason
- icp_confidence

Allowed fit values:

- strong_fit
- moderate_fit
- weak_fit
- disqualified

## GTM Signals

- signal_type
- signal_description
- signal_date
- signal_strength
- signal_source
- source_url

Examples:

- hiring
- funding
- expansion
- leadership_change
- new_market
- product_launch
- technology_change
- sales_team_growth
- revops_hiring
- crm_or_automation_change

## Evidence Layer

Each meaningful claim should include:

- evidence_text
- evidence_source
- evidence_url
- evidence_date
- evidence_quality

Evidence quality:

- high
- medium
- low

## AI Analysis

- likely_gtm_problem
- problem_hypothesis
- why_now
- relevance_to_offer
- confidence
- uncertainty_notes

## Persona Selection

- primary_persona
- secondary_persona
- persona_reason

Examples:

- VP Sales
- Head of RevOps
- Revenue Operations Manager
- GTM Operations
- Sales Operations
- COO
- Founder

## Messaging Intelligence

- account_angle
- evidence_backed_hook
- problem_hypothesis
- value_hypothesis
- risk_or_objection
- recommended_next_step

## Prioritization

- fit_score
- signal_score
- evidence_score
- timing_score
- total_priority_score
- priority_tier

Priority tiers:

- P1
- P2
- P3
- Review
